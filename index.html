FAQ

Common questions about using Najm ICUCalc safely.

Is Najm ICUCalc a substitute for clinical judgment?

No. It is a reference aid that displays standard weight-based ranges. Always verify against your institution's protocols and the patient's clinical context before administering any drug.

Which dosing units are used?

Most continuous infusions are shown as mcg/kg/min, mg/kg/hr, or mg/min, matching common ICU references. The unit is shown alongside every range.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, the app is fully client-side and continues to work without internet. You can add it to your home screen as a PWA.

Can I suggest a drug or correction?

Yes — open an issue on the repository or submit a pull request. Every change is reviewed by the maintainers.
