THE PROBLEM ASH SOLVES

Hajj medicine is the world's
highest-stakes sprint.

Saudi MOH treats over 200,000 cardiac and heat-related cases across the five days of Hajj. The window for STEMI reperfusion shrinks to single digits. Polyglot triage burns minutes. And the existing apps were built for offices, not for a physician running between tents at 47 °C with one usable thumb. ASH redesigns the entire flow around that physician.

01 · CORE

Two-tap rule

Every life-saving action — STEMI activation, drug dose, ACLS branch — reachable inside two taps from any screen, by an exhausted thumb.

02 · CORE

Cited or silent

No answer ships without source (AHA, ESC, SCCM, MOH-KSA), publication year, level of evidence and a calibrated confidence bar. AI never hand-waves.

03 · CORE

Polyglot by default

Voice triage in Arabic, English, Urdu, Indonesian, Turkish, French, Farsi. The pilgrim speaks; ASH listens and prints clinical English for you.

04 · CORE

Hajj-aware

WBGT heat stress, Jamarat crowd density, prayer-time silencing, Tawakkalna pilgrim ID, and KAMC/NGHA referral status — first-class data, not afterthoughts.

05 · CORE

Offline-first

Every protocol, dose, and calculator runs without network. ASH is a survival tool when 5G dies in Mina — sync resumes when you breathe again.

09 · SHIFT TASLEEM

The handover that
writes itself.

"Tasleem" — the act of handing over. ASH watches your shift: every order, every protocol step, every note. At end-of-shift it drafts a one-page handover in your style — sector status, beds of concern, pending tasks, escalation lines — and waits for you to sign. Most shifts: 8 seconds of editing. Zero forgotten patients.

Shift-watch summarisation
Auto-built from your timeline, not free-typed. Every claim links to the timestamped action.
Pending-task carry-over
Open trops, pending bloods, CT slots: each migrates to the next physician with countdowns intact.
Signed, audited, archived
PDF + MOH-KSA structured JSON. Surveys to your tablet next shift; available to QI in seconds.
10 · SAFETY NET

The voice that says
"don't."

The Safety Net is the layer that never sleeps. It watches every dose, every order, every protocol branch — and intervenes only when the cost of silence is too high. It is calibrated to interrupt rarely and interrupt loudly when it must. Alarm fatigue is treated as a clinical injury.

Three-tier alert grammar
Critical (vital-red, blocks action) · Warn (solar, requires confirm) · Inform (bismuth, soft tap). No 4th tier ever.
Cited refusal
If ASH blocks a dose, it explains why with the citation (KDIGO, ESC, AHA) and offers the closest safe alternative.
Decision audit log
Every override is logged with reason. Medico-legally complete. QI-team gold.
